Delete your Rally account

Rally, by Markus Strømnes

In the app (instant)

  1. Open Rally and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete account at the bottom.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted immediately.

Without the app

Email marstrom96@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my Rally account”. Your account will be deleted within 7 days and you will get a confirmation reply.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email address and password)
  • Your profile: display name and goal weight
  • All your weigh-ins, and with them your streaks and rankings
  • Your memberships in groups

Groups you created are handed to the member who joined earliest, so your friends keep their group. A group you are the last member of is deleted. Nothing is kept after deletion, apart from the hosting provider's backups, which expire on their normal schedule.

See also the privacy policy.
