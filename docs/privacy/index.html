Rally

Privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

Rally is a weight-tracking app for small groups of friends, made by Markus Strømnes as a private, non-commercial project. This policy explains what the app stores, who can see it and how to get it deleted.

What Rally stores

  • Account: your email address and a password (stored only as a secure hash).
  • Profile: the display name you choose and, if you set one, your goal weight.
  • Weigh-ins: your weight and the date of each weigh-in. This is health information.
  • Groups: the groups you create or join, their names and invite codes.

Some settings are stored only on your phone and never sent to us: your daily reminder time, your colour and light/dark choices, and your sign-in session.

Who can see your data

  • You can see all of your own data.
  • Members of a group you are in can see your display name, your weigh-ins and weight changes, your streaks, and whether you have weighed in today. Rally is built around sharing progress with friends; only join groups with people you want to share this with. Leave a group to stop sharing with it.
  • Nobody else. Rally has no ads and no analytics or tracking, and your data is never sold or shared for marketing.

Services Rally uses

  • Supabase hosts the database and handles sign-in. Your account and app data are stored there.
  • Expo delivers app updates to your phone. Downloading an update involves your device's IP address and app version, but no personal data from Rally.

These providers process data only to run the app. Reminders are local notifications created on your phone; no push service is used.

Why it is stored

Only to provide the app: showing your trend, goal and streaks, and sharing progress inside your groups. The legal basis is your consent when you create an account and choose to log weigh-ins and join groups.

How long it is kept

Until you delete your account. Deleting it removes your profile, settings, weigh-ins and group memberships straight away. Groups you own are handed to another member; a group you are the last member of is deleted. Backups held by the hosting provider expire on their normal schedule.

Your rights

You can see and change your data in the app, and delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete account, or by following the steps on the account deletion page. You can also ask for a copy of your data, or for anything to be corrected or deleted, by emailing marstrom96@gmail.com. If you are in Norway you can complain to Datatilsynet; elsewhere in the EEA, to your local data protection authority.

Children

Rally is not intended for anyone under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
